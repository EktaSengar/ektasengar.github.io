Bluff.ai — quality monitoring for AI support agents

Problem.
Companies are handing customer support to AI agents, but teams still review only a tiny sample of what those agents say. A made-up price, a promise that breaks policy, or a cold reply to a frustrated customer can go unnoticed until it shows up as churn, a refund or a compliance issue. Leaders have no reliable way to know whether their AI agent can be trusted.
Solution.
Continuous quality monitoring that reviews AI support conversations against the company's own policies and flags the ones that need a human.
Stage.
Early validation; more to share soon.
My role.
Founder. I own it end to end: problem and customer discovery, product vision and strategy, pricing and go-to-market, and hands-on building of the prototype.

Coucou — French from A1 to B2, built while learning it

Problem.
Adult learners moving to France juggle classes, apps and AI chatbots with no single path to the exam-ready B2 level.
Solution.
A free, structured course that pairs short lessons with native-voice audio, dictation, shadowing and oral practice, plus AI-tutor prompts for speaking practice.
Results.
Live with 73 lessons across A1–B2, shaped week by week by my own French classes.
My role.
Founder. I design AI agents around my own needs, and this one started with mine: learning French for life in Paris. I own it end to end, from curriculum and learning experience to the audio pipeline and the build, and I'm its first user.

Allez.city — a city guide for the people who live there

Problem.
City guides are written for tourists and event listings are endless; residents just want to know what's worth doing next, nearby.
Solution.
A short, ranked set of suggestions that adapts to weather, date and location, each with a human reason to care, running on one engine with a pack per city.
Results.
Live in 5 cities across 3 continents; a native mobile app is in development.
My role.
Founder. Another AI agent built for my own life: exploring the city when I'm not working, because going out shouldn't be a hassle. I own it end to end: product vision, ranking logic, editorial voice, data sourcing, and the hands-on build of the web engine and mobile app.
